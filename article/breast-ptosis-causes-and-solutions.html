Breast Ptosis Causes and Solutions — Understanding Breast Sagging

Reviewed by our editorial team · Updated May 2026 · 6 min read

Breast ptosis, commonly known as breast sagging, is a natural condition that affects women of all ages. While it is often associated with aging, breast ptosis can result from a variety of factors including genetics, pregnancy, weight fluctuations, and hormonal changes.

Understanding the causes of breast sagging and available management options can help you make informed decisions about your breast health. Use our free breast ptosis test to assess your current sagging level, or read on to learn more.

What Is Breast Ptosis?

Breast ptosis is the medical term for breast sagging or drooping. It occurs when the skin and supporting ligaments of the breast lose elasticity, causing the breast tissue to descend. The condition is classified using the Regnault classification system, which grades ptosis based on the position of the nipple relative to the breast crease (inframammary fold):

  • Grade 0 (No ptosis): Nipple is above the breast crease
  • Grade 1 (Mild ptosis): Nipple is at the level of the breast crease
  • Grade 2 (Moderate ptosis): Nipple is below the crease but above the lowest breast contour
  • Grade 3 (Advanced ptosis): Nipple is at the lowest point of the breast, pointing downward
  • Grade 4 (Severe ptosis): Nipple is well below the lowest breast contour

You can use our breast ptosis assessment tool to determine your grade and get personalized recommendations.

Common Causes of Breast Ptosis

Aging and Skin Elasticity

As women age, the skin naturally loses collagen and elastin, the proteins responsible for skin firmness and elasticity. This natural aging process causes the skin to become looser, which can lead to breast sagging over time. The Cooper's ligaments that support breast tissue also stretch with age.

Pregnancy and Breastfeeding

During pregnancy, hormonal changes cause the breasts to enlarge as milk-producing glands develop. After pregnancy and breastfeeding, these glands shrink, which can leave the breast tissue less full and the skin stretched. Multiple pregnancies can increase the likelihood of breast ptosis.

Significant Weight Changes

Weight gain causes the breasts to stretch as they increase in size, while weight loss reduces breast volume. Repeated cycles of weight gain and loss can progressively reduce skin elasticity, leading to sagging. Maintaining a stable, healthy weight can help minimize breast ptosis.

Genetics and Breast Composition

Genetics play a significant role in determining breast size, shape, and density. Women with larger, denser breasts are more prone to sagging because heavier breast tissue places more strain on the supporting ligaments. Breast composition is largely determined by genetic factors.

Smoking and Lifestyle Factors

Smoking accelerates skin aging by breaking down collagen and reducing blood flow to the skin. This can contribute to premature breast sagging. A healthy lifestyle with proper nutrition, hydration, and sun protection can help maintain skin elasticity.

Prevention Strategies for Breast Sagging

While some causes of breast ptosis are unavoidable, there are steps you can take to minimize sagging:

  • Wear a properly fitted bra: A supportive bra reduces strain on the Cooper's ligaments. Use our bra size calculator to find your correct size
  • Maintain a stable weight: Avoid significant weight fluctuations that can stretch the skin
  • Stay hydrated: Proper hydration helps maintain skin elasticity
  • Moisturize and protect skin: Use moisturizers containing collagen-boosting ingredients
  • Exercise chest muscles: Strengthening the pectoral muscles underneath breast tissue can improve the appearance of the bust
  • Avoid smoking: Smoking accelerates collagen breakdown and skin aging

Management Options for Breast Ptosis

If you are concerned about breast sagging, several management options are available depending on the severity:

  • Proper bra fitting: A well-fitted bra can dramatically improve the appearance and comfort of sagging breasts. Use our bra size calculator to find your ideal fit
  • Supportive bra styles: Full-coverage bras, minimizer bras, and bras with wider straps provide additional support
  • Strengthening exercises: Push-ups, chest presses, and dumbbell flys strengthen the pectoral muscles
  • Topical treatments: Creams containing retinol or peptides may improve skin firmness
  • Cosmetic procedures: For advanced ptosis, breast lift surgery (mastopexy) is an option — consult a board-certified plastic surgeon

Breast ptosis is a natural part of life for many women. Understanding its causes and knowing your options empowers you to make the best choices for your body. Start by taking our free breast ptosis test to assess your current condition.
