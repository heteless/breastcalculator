Breast Volume Guide — How to Estimate and Understand Your Breast Volume

By Dr Cryptomeria, PhD in Fashion Design · May 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Breast volume is a measurement of the amount of breast tissue in cubic centimeters (cc). While bra size tells you which band and cup combination to look for, breast volume gives you a more precise understanding of your breast size in three-dimensional space. This information can be valuable for bra fitting, assessing breast symmetry, and planning for cosmetic or reconstructive procedures.

In this guide, we explain how breast volume is estimated, what different volume ranges mean, and how you can use this information for your breast health journey.

What Is Breast Volume?

Breast volume refers to the total amount of tissue in each breast, measured in cubic centimeters (cc) or milliliters (ml). Unlike bra cup size — which is a relative measurement based on the difference between band and bust circumference — breast volume is an absolute measurement that tells you the actual size of the breast.

Key differences between breast volume and bra size:

  • Bra size — A relative sizing system (e.g., 34C) that varies between brands and countries
  • Breast volume — An objective measurement in cc that remains constant regardless of brand
  • Breast volume is especially useful for medical purposes, post-surgery planning, and symmetry assessment

How Is Breast Volume Estimated?

Breast volume can be estimated using several methods, from simple at-home calculations to advanced 3D imaging in clinical settings.

  1. Circumference Method (At-Home): Using your underbust and bust measurements, breast volume can be estimated by calculating the difference between the two measurements and applying a formula. While not as precise as clinical methods, this provides a useful approximation.
  2. Water Displacement: A clinical method where the breast is submerged in water, and the displaced water volume equals the breast volume. This is rarely used in modern practice.
  3. 3D Scanning / MRI: The most accurate method. Advanced imaging creates a 3D model of the breast to calculate exact volume. Used primarily for surgical planning.

Our free breast volume estimation tool uses the circumference method to give you an approximate breast volume range based on your measurements.

Breast Volume and Implant Sizing

For those considering breast augmentation, reconstruction after mastectomy, or reduction surgery, understanding breast volume is essential. Surgeons use volume measurements in cc to plan procedures:

  • Augmentation: Implants typically range from 150 cc to 800 cc. Your existing breast volume helps determine the implant size needed to achieve your desired result.
  • Reduction: Surgeons remove a specific volume of tissue (e.g., 300-500 cc per breast) to achieve a smaller, more comfortable size.
  • Reconstruction: After mastectomy, reconstruction aims to match the volume of the remaining breast for symmetry.

Breast Volume and Symmetry

It is completely normal for one breast to be slightly larger than the other. Studies show that most women have some degree of breast asymmetry. Here is what the volume difference typically means:

  • Less than 10% difference: Very common, usually not noticeable
  • 10-20% difference: Mild asymmetry, may be noticeable in fitted clothing
  • More than 20% difference: Significant asymmetry; custom bra fitting or consultation with a specialist may be helpful

How to Use Breast Volume for Bra Fitting

Knowing your approximate breast volume can help you make better bra choices:

  • Choose bras with appropriate cup depth and width for your volume
  • Identify when sister sizes provide a better fit for your specific volume distribution
  • Select bra styles suited to your volume (e.g., balconette for smaller volume, full-cup for larger volume)
  • Track volume changes over time due to weight changes, pregnancy, or aging

Frequently Asked Questions

Is breast volume the same as cup size?

Not exactly. While cup size is related to volume, it is a relative measurement within a sizing system that varies between brands. Breast volume in cc is an absolute measurement that gives a more precise picture of breast size.

Can I measure breast volume at home accurately?

While at-home circumference methods provide a useful estimate, they are not as accurate as 3D imaging or MRI. For general bra fitting and health awareness, at-home estimates are sufficient. For surgical planning, professional clinical measurements are essential.

Does breast volume change over time?

Yes. Breast volume can change due to weight fluctuations, pregnancy, breastfeeding, menopause, hormonal changes, and aging. Regular measurement can help you track these natural changes.

What is the average breast volume?

Average breast volume varies by population and body type. In the United States, the average breast volume for women is approximately 300-400 cc per breast, corresponding roughly to a C cup size on a 34 band.
