    Why Breast Health Matters

    Breast ptosis (sagging) is a common condition that affects women of all ages. While it is a natural part of aging, it can also be caused by factors such as pregnancy, breastfeeding, weight fluctuations, and improper bra support.

    Understanding your breast ptosis level is important because:

    • It helps you choose the right bra for proper support
    • It allows you to track changes in your breast health over time
    • It helps you identify when to consult a healthcare professional
    • It empowers you to make informed decisions about your body

    Regular breast self-exams and proper bra fitting are essential components of breast health. Use our tools above to better understand your body and take proactive steps toward maintaining healthy breasts.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I measure my underbust?

    Wrap a measuring tape snugly around your ribcage, directly under your bust. Make sure the tape is parallel to the ground and snug but not tight. Round to the nearest half inch.

    How do I measure my bust?

    Wrap the measuring tape around the fullest part of your bust, keeping it parallel to the ground. Do not pull too tight. Round to the nearest half inch for the most accurate result.

    What is breast ptosis?

    Breast ptosis is the medical term for breast sagging. It occurs when the breasts lose firmness and elasticity, causing them to droop. This is measured by the position of the nipple relative to the breast crease.

    Can exercise prevent breast sagging?

    While exercise cannot directly lift breasts (which contain no muscle tissue), strengthening the pectoral muscles underneath can improve the appearance of the chest. Wearing proper support during exercise is also essential.

    When should I see a doctor about breast sagging?

    If you experience sudden changes in breast shape or position, pain, skin changes, or if you have concerns about your breast health, consult a healthcare professional. Our calculator is for educational purposes only and does not replace medical advice.
