Wellness Header Pop-up

Reference implementation: condensed clickable article cards inside the WELLNESS header dropdown.

↥ Hover or focus the WELLNESS nav item above to open the pop-up.

Design decisions

  • Card pattern source: mirrors .article-card from /articles/ — same padding scale, border treatment, hover lift ( translateY(-2px) + --shadow-hover ), and color tokens.
  • Condensed display: cards use 2-line -webkit-line-clamp on title and blurb, plus a 60vh max-height with internal scroll on the grid (12 articles fit without overflow on desktop).
  • Visual clickable cues: left-edge accent bar that scales in on hover, arrow → that nudges right, tag color shift, and dashed meta separator.
  • Typography hierarchy: Playfair Display titles (0.92rem) → Inter body (0.78rem) → muted meta (0.7rem uppercase tag, 0.7rem time).
  • Responsive: 2-column grid on tablet+desktop, single column on mobile; featured card collapses from 3-column to 1-column layout under 768px.
  • Performance: pure CSS, no JS, no images, no extra HTTP requests. Rendered inline with the navbar.
  • Accessibility: role="menu" + role="menuitem" , focusable cards (native <a> elements), keyboard activation, aria-label on the pop-up container.
