How does weight conversion work?

All weight conversions use internationally agreed standard definitions. The base unit is the kilogram (kg) in the SI system; every other unit in this tool is defined by an exact conversion factor to kilograms.

  • 1 kg = 1000 g = 1,000,000 mg = 2.20462 lb = 35.274 oz (NIST)
  • 1 lb = 16 oz = 453.592 g = 0.453592 kg (international avoirdupois)
  • 1 stone (st) = 14 lb = 6.35029 kg (UK body weight unit)
  • 1 oz = 28.3495 g = 0.0283495 kg (avoirdupois)

How to Use the Professional Weight Converter

This body weight converter handles 6 common weight units from the metric, US customary, and UK imperial systems. It is especially useful for personal weight tracking, breast weight estimates, garment load analysis, and clinical documentation.

Step 1 — Choose your settings: Select the number of decimal places (2, 3, or 4) and your preferred rounding mode (standard, banker's, or truncate). The default 2-decimal mode is perfect for everyday use.

Step 2 — Enter your weight: Type the numeric value in the input field. You can enter integers (70), decimals (70.5), or scientific notation (7e2 = 700). The converter handles all of these natively.

Step 3 — Select units: Use the From and To dropdowns to choose your source and target units. Both dropdowns are grouped (Metric, US Customary, UK Imperial) so you can quickly find the right unit.

Step 4 — Read your result: The result appears instantly. For best accuracy, switch to 3 or 4 decimal places when comparing across unit systems (e.g., metric to UK stones). Save the result via the Print, Recalculate, or Share buttons.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the weight conversion?

All conversions use international standard definitions. 1 kg = 2.2046226218 lb exactly (NIST). 1 lb = 16 oz = 453.592 g exactly. 1 stone (UK) = 14 lb = 6.35029318 kg exactly. Conversions are mathematically exact to floating-point precision.

What is a stone (st)?

A stone is a traditional British unit of body weight equal to 14 pounds (6.35029318 kg). It is still commonly used in the UK and Ireland for personal weight reporting. For example, 11 stone = 154 lb = 69.85 kg.

Which units does this converter support?

This converter focuses on body-scale units: milligrams (mg), grams (g), kilograms (kg), ounces (oz), pounds (lb), and stones (st). Larger units like metric tons, US/UK tons, troy ounces, and carats are excluded because they fall outside the body-weight range.

Why do I need milligrams for body weight?

Milligrams are useful for tracking the weight of small body structures (lymph nodes, cysts, breast tissue biopsy samples) and for research-grade precision in anthropometric studies. Most users will only need kg, lb, and st.

Can I convert negative or zero values?

The converter accepts any numeric input. Zero converts to zero in all units. Negative values convert to their negative equivalents.

What is the formula for weight conversion?

The standard formulas use NIST-defined factors. To convert kg to lb: lb = kg × 2.2046226218 . To convert lb to kg: kg = lb × 0.45359237 . To convert stone to kg: kg = st × 6.35029318 .

How do I convert pounds to kilograms?

To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply the pound value by 0.45359237. For example, 150 lb × 0.45359237 = 68.04 kg. To convert kg to lb, divide by 0.45359237 or multiply by 2.2046226218.

Why is the stone (st) still used in body weight?

The stone is a traditional British unit of body weight (1 stone = 14 lb = 6.35 kg) still widely used in the UK and Ireland for personal weight reporting. It is not used in the US or in scientific literature, where pounds and kilograms are standard.
