About Breast Calculator

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Our Mission

Breast Calculator was created with a simple mission: to provide free, accurate, and accessible breast health measurement tools for every woman, regardless of her location or financial situation. We believe that understanding your body is the first step toward taking control of your health.

Who We Are

We are a team of health enthusiasts, web developers, and women's wellness advocates dedicated to creating reliable digital tools that help women better understand their bodies. Our calculators are designed based on widely accepted medical guidelines and measurement standards.

What We Offer

  • Breast Size Calculator — Accurately determine your bra size using standard underbust and bust measurements, including sister size alternatives.
  • Breast Ptosis (Sagging) Test — Assess your breast ptosis level based on recognized medical grading criteria and receive personalized recommendations.
  • Educational Resources — Learn about breast health, proper bra fitting, and when to seek professional medical advice.

Our Values

  • Accuracy — Our tools are built on established medical measurement standards and regularly reviewed for correctness.
  • Privacy — We respect your privacy. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. No personal data is ever stored or transmitted to our servers.
  • Accessibility — Our tools are completely free to use, with no registration required. We are committed to keeping them that way.
  • Education — We provide clear explanations and actionable recommendations to help you make informed decisions about your health.

Medical Disclaimer

Breast Calculator is an educational tool designed for informational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The results from our calculators should not be used as a substitute for professional medical consultation. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for any breast health concerns or before making decisions about your health.

Contact Us

We value your feedback and suggestions. If you have any questions, comments, or ideas for improving our tools, please reach out to us at support@breastcalculator.com. We strive to respond to all inquiries within 48 hours.
