Prosthetic Bras Guide

A gentle introduction to finding comfort, confidence, and the right fit after mastectomy.

After mastectomy, many women choose to wear a breast form (prosthesis) to restore symmetry and balance. A prosthetic bra is specifically designed to hold these forms securely and comfortably. This guide offers a warm, practical introduction to help you navigate your options with confidence.

What Is a Prosthetic Bra?

A prosthetic bra is a specially designed bra with built-in pockets that hold a breast form securely against the chest. These bras are crafted with soft fabrics, wider straps for comfort, and front-closure options for ease of dressing. They are available for both unilateral (one side) and bilateral (both sides) use.

Key Features to Look For

Pocketed Cups

The most important feature is a secure, well-designed pocket that holds the breast form in place without shifting. Look for bras with full-pocket or partial-pocket designs that match the type of prosthesis you use.

Soft, Breathable Fabrics

Post-surgical skin can be sensitive. Choose bras made from cotton, bamboo, or microfiber blends that are gentle against scars and healing tissue. Seamless or flat-seam construction reduces irritation.

Wide, Adjustable Straps

Wider straps distribute weight more evenly, reducing pressure on the shoulders. Adjustable straps allow you to customize the fit as your body changes during recovery.

Front Closure Options

Front-closure bras (with hooks or zippers) can be much easier to put on and take off, especially during the early recovery period when arm mobility may be limited.

Finding Your Fit

Getting properly fitted is essential. A certified mastectomy fitter can measure you and help you choose the right bra and breast form combination. Many specialty lingerie stores and medical supply shops offer fitting services. Your bra band should feel snug but not tight, and the breast form should sit naturally against your chest without gaping or shifting.

Styles to Consider

  • Everyday Bras: Soft, comfortable bras for daily wear with full pocket support.
  • Sports Bras: Designed for active wear, offering gentle compression and pocketed support for breast forms during exercise.
  • Sleep Bras: Lightweight, wire-free bras that provide gentle support and pocket security during rest.
  • Swimwear: Water-resistant prosthetic bras with drainage features for swimming and beach activities.

FAQs

How do I know what size breast form I need?

A certified mastectomy fitter can help you determine the right size and shape. The goal is to match the weight, size, and shape of your remaining breast for natural symmetry. Many women find that their breast form size corresponds closely to their pre-surgery bra cup size.

Can I wear a regular bra after mastectomy?

Some women choose to wear regular bras without breast forms, especially if they do not desire symmetry. However, if you use a breast form, a pocketed prosthetic bra is recommended to keep the form securely in place. Regular bras can be modified by a seamstress to add internal pockets.
